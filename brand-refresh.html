About This Project

This brand refresh project involved a complete visual identity overhaul for a client seeking to modernize their brand and stand out in a competitive market. Every element was redesigned from the ground up to create a cohesive, memorable, and professional brand identity.

The goal was to move away from outdated visuals and establish a fresh, contemporary look that resonates with the target audience while maintaining the core values and mission of the business.

What We Delivered

  • Logo Design — A versatile primary logo, secondary mark, and icon that work across all media.
  • Color Palette — A carefully curated set of primary and secondary colors that evoke the right emotions and ensure consistency.
  • Typography — Selected font pairings that balance readability with personality across print and digital.
  • Brand Guidelines — A comprehensive document covering logo usage, color codes, typography, imagery style, and application examples.
  • Stationery Suite — Business cards, letterheads, and email signatures aligned with the new identity.
  • Social Media Kit — Profile pictures, cover images, and template designs for all major platforms.

Design Approach

The design process began with in-depth research into the client's industry, competitors, and target audience. Mood boards and concept sketches were presented, refined through feedback rounds, and delivered as a polished, cohesive brand system. Every color, shape, and font was chosen with intention to communicate the brand's unique personality and values.

Project Impact

The refreshed brand identity helped the client achieve a more professional and trustworthy presence across all touchpoints. Customer recognition improved, and the client reported increased engagement on social media and higher conversion rates on marketing materials.
